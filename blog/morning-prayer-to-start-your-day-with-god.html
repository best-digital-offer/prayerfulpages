Morning Prayer to Start Your Day With God

Quick answer: A morning prayer can help Christians begin the day with gratitude, wisdom, peace, strength, and intentional time with God.

Why this matters

A morning prayer is a prayer offered at the beginning of the day. It can include gratitude, guidance, strength, protection, family, work, and surrender.

A simple prayer or starting point

Try this short prayer: “Father, thank You for this new day. Give me wisdom for my decisions, patience in my conversations, courage for what I cannot control, and a heart that notices opportunities to do good. Amen.”

Practical guidance

Useful morning themes include gratitude, guidance, strength, protection, wisdom, and peace. Choose one or two rather than trying to cover everything.

Build a sustainable habit

For a 10-minute routine, spend two minutes becoming quiet, three minutes reading Scripture, three minutes praying through priorities, and two minutes in gratitude.

Frequently Asked Question

What is a good morning prayer?

One that is honest, focused, and connected to your faith. There is no required length or special formula.
