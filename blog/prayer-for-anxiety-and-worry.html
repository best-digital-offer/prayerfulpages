Prayer for Anxiety and Worry: A Christian Guide to Finding Peace

Quick answer: A gentle Christian guide to praying through anxiety and worry, with Scripture themes and a short prayer.

Why this matters

When worry feels overwhelming, keep prayer simple. Name the concern, acknowledge your feelings, ask God for peace and wisdom, and focus on the next faithful step.

A simple prayer or starting point

“Lord, You know what is troubling me. Help me slow down, remember that I am not alone, and place today's concerns in Your hands. Give me wisdom about what I can do and peace about what I cannot control. Amen.”

Practical guidance

Christians often use Scripture themes about God's presence, peace, courage, trust, and care when praying through worry.

Build a sustainable habit

A five-minute routine can include quiet breathing, naming the main concern, reading a short passage, praying honestly, and ending with gratitude. Persistent or severe anxiety also deserves appropriate professional support.

Frequently Asked Question

Can prayer replace professional help for anxiety?

No. Prayer can be part of spiritual practice, but persistent or severe anxiety should also receive appropriate professional attention.
