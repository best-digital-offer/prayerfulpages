Communion Prayer: Preparing Your Heart Before and After the Table

In this guide: Explore a thoughtful communion prayer, ways to prepare for the Lord's Supper, and reflection prompts for before and after receiving Communion.

A communion prayer can help a person approach the Lord's Supper with gratitude, remembrance, humility, and attention to the meaning their church tradition gives this practice. Churches differ in theology and preparation, so use this guide alongside your own community's teaching.

An original prayer before Communion

Lord Jesus, help me approach this meal with gratitude and a sincere heart. Bring to mind Your self-giving love, teach me to examine my life honestly, and help me seek peace where I have caused harm. Thank You for the grace and hope at the centre of the Christian story. Strengthen me to love my neighbour, serve with humility, and remember those who are overlooked. May this time of remembrance shape the way I live after I leave the table. Amen.

This is an original devotional example, not a replacement for a church's prescribed liturgy or pastoral direction.

Prepare with remembrance, gratitude, and honesty

Many Christian communities encourage worshippers to reflect on the significance of Jesus' death and resurrection, give thanks, and consider their relationships with God and others. Some traditions emphasise confession and reconciliation before receiving Communion. Follow the guidance of your congregation, especially if your church has specific teaching about who may receive the elements and how to prepare.

Preparation should invite honest reflection, not panic or perfectionism. If you have a question about your church's practice, asking a pastor or priest is more reliable than relying on a generic online explanation.

Questions to reflect on before the service

  • What am I grateful for today?
  • Is there an apology, act of repair, or conversation I need to consider?
  • Who in my community needs compassion, practical help, or prayer?
  • What truth about Jesus do I want to remember as I participate?

You do not need to solve every concern in a few minutes. Let these questions direct your attention and identify a practical next step where appropriate.

A prayer of thanksgiving after Communion

Gracious God, thank You for meeting Your people in worship and for the hope we remember at the table. Help me carry this gratitude into daily life. Where I have been impatient, teach me to listen. Where I have been comfortable ignoring another person's need, move me toward generosity. Where I have received mercy, help me practise mercy. Keep me connected to the church community and ready to serve in ordinary ways. Amen.

Make the reflection part of a lasting habit

After the service, write one sentence about what you want to remember and one practical way to live it out during the week. This can be as simple as checking in on a neighbour, setting aside time for prayer, or making a sincere apology. A prayer journal or devotional can help you return to the same themes, but it should support participation in community rather than replace it.

Frequently asked questions

What should I pray before Communion?

You can pray with gratitude, remembrance, repentance where needed, and a desire to live out Christ's love. Follow your church's guidance about preparation.

Is there one communion prayer used by every church?

No. Communion liturgies and beliefs differ among Christian traditions. This page offers a personal reflection, not a universal prescribed text.
