Family Prayer: Simple Christian Prayers for Children and Home

Quick answer: Ideas for family prayer, including short prayers for children, home, school, decisions, protection, and gratitude.

Why this matters

Family prayer is a shared or individual practice of bringing family needs, gratitude, decisions, and hopes to God. It can be formal or very simple.

A simple prayer or starting point

“Father, thank You for our family and this home. Give us patience with one another, wisdom in our decisions, protection in our days, and humility to say sorry and forgive. Amen.”

Practical guidance

Start with one or two minutes at a predictable time such as before a meal or bedtime. Let children contribute one thing they are thankful for or one person they want to pray for.

Build a sustainable habit

Parents can pray for children's character, friendships, learning, safety, confidence, choices, and ability to grow in wisdom and compassion.

Frequently Asked Question

How long should family prayer be?

There is no fixed length. A few focused minutes can be enough to establish a routine.
